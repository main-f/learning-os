# Product feedback

## 2026-11-12 — P0-B.1 Game Feel, Randomization & Chinese Assistance

1. Correct answers need a light, fast positive-feedback effect.
2. The game timer must display milliseconds.
3. Visuals and interaction may draw from the general language of Duolingo-style learning games, but must not copy its name, trademarks, mascot, illustrations, or proprietary assets.
4. Every session randomizes question order; every question also randomizes its four option positions for that session.
5. Before starting, the user may choose Chinese translation assistance. Assisted sessions use a `1.3` ranked-time multiplier while always retaining the original English.

These decisions extend the existing private real-MCQ slice. They do not authorize Telegram, NotebookLM, remote deployment, GradeCalc, or additional game modes.
